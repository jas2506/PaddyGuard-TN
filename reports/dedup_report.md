## Settings

- Perceptual hash: `imagehash.phash`, hash_size 8 (64 bit), JPEGs decoded at reduced size (draft 256 px), EXIF rotation applied. A 64-bit phash always has 32 set bits, so only even distances occur.
- Near-duplicate thresholds (Hamming distance, `dedup.hamming`): S <= 8, T1 <= 14, T2 <= 4
- Cross-dataset leakage threshold (`dedup.leakage_hamming`): <= 8
- Review of the boundary pairs: Boundary pairs were reviewed visually during implementation (not by the developer; the sheet is kept for a re-check). S at 8: pairs at 8-10 are near-identical shots of the same plot, so grouping at 8 is the conservative choice. T1 at 14: pairs at 8-14 are shifted or zoomed copies of the same leaf; pairs just above 14 were not inspected, so some duplicates may remain at larger distances. T2 at 4: pairs at 4 are repeat shots of the same leaf, while pairs at 6 mix repeats and different leaves on the same plain background (the threshold 8 merged different leaves into one chain of 33). Cross-dataset pairs at 10-12 are visibly unrelated photos, so no leakage was found at the leakage threshold of 8.
